---
author: Johann C. Lavater
category: Seeking the Lord—For Growth in Him尋求主——求在祂裏長大
composer: Old English Air
meter: 8.6.8.6.D.
title: O Jesus Christ, grow Thou in me
tune: Prospect
---

# 1

O Jesus Christ, grow Thou in me,
哦主，求祢長在我心，
And all things else recede;
祢外再無他求！
My heart be daily nearer Thee,
使我逐日與祢更親，
From sin be daily freed.
逐日向罪自由。

# 1-chorus

Each day let Thy supporting might
願祢逐日維持的力，
My weakness still embrace;
仍然顧我軟弱，
My darkness vanish in Thy light,
祢的亮光除我陰翳，
Thy life my death efface.
生命吞我死涸。

# 2

In Thy bright beams which on me fall,
當祢榮光照我心頭，
Fade every evil thought;
所有惡念都消；
That I am nothing, Thou art all,
“我是無有，祢是萬有”，
I would be daily taught.
我願常受此敎。

# 3

More of Thy glory let me see,
祢這聖潔、榮耀的主，
Thou Holy, Wise, and True;
讓我更多瞻仰；
I would Thy living image be,
無論快樂或是痛苦，
In joy and sorrow too.
我願作祢活像。

# 4

Fill me with gladness from above,
天上喜樂，求祢膏我，
Hold me by strength divine;
靈力，求祢助我；
Lord, let the glow of Thy great love
但願祢的熱切愛火，
Through all my being shine.
從我全人射過。

# 5

Make this poor self grow less and less,
可憐的己，願其消沉，
Be Thou my life and aim;
惟祢作我目標，
Oh, make me daily through Thy grace
使我逐日藉着祢恩，
More meet to bear Thy name.
更配與祢相交。
