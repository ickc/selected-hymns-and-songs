# 1

Live in the light of life,
活在生命光中，
Sweet fellowship maintain;
不斷與主交通，
Behold His gracious face,
瞻仰恩主慈容，
Each day more glory gain.
順從聖靈感動，
The Spirit’s move obey,
天天榮上加榮，
Rejoice and praise and pray;
時時讚美、稱頌，
Live in the light of life.
活在生命光中。
