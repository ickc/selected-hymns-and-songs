# 1

Emmanuel, Emmanuel;
以馬內利，以馬內利，
His name is called Emmanuel.
祂名稱爲以馬內利。
God is with us, revealed in us;
神在我裏，與我同住，
His name is called Emmanuel.
祂名稱爲以馬內利。
