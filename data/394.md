# 1

Not my will but Thine be done,
非我意願，乃祢旨，
But let the fullness of the Son
得以成全；讓主豐滿，
Rule within this life that I have offered Thee,
在我裏面，作王掌權；
Until everything I do
阿爸、父阿，我將此生全奉獻；
Becomes the thing that pleases You,
願我所作，討祢心歡；
Abba Father I would be a son indeed.
直等到，兒子實際，從我顯現！
