# 1

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace,
凡堅心倚賴祢的人，
Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace,
祢必保守，十分平安；
Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace,
祢必保守，十分平安；
Whose mind is stayed on Thee.
因爲他倚靠祢。
