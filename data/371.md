category: Seeking the Lord—Needing Him尋求主——需要祂
composer: Robert Lowry
meter:
  en: 6.4.6.4. with chorus
  zh: 6.4.6.4. 和
title: I need Thee ev’ry hour
tune: I Need Thee
---

# 1

I need Thee every hour,
我每時刻需你，
Most gracious Lord;
可愛恩主；
No tender voice like Thine
無人柔聲似你，
Can peace afford.
能施安撫。

# 1-chorus

I need Thee, oh, I need Thee;
我需你，主，我需祢，
Every hour I need Thee;
每時刻我需你；
Oh, bless me now, my Savior!
哦主，現在賜恩力，
I come to Thee.
我來就祢。

# 2

I need Thee every hour,
我每時刻需祢，
Stay Thou near by;
求你來臨；
Temptations lose their power
試探失去能力，
When Thou art nigh.
當祢親近。

# 3

I need Thee every hour,
我每時刻需祢，
In joy or pain;
或樂或苦；
Come quickly and abide,
速來住留不離，
Or life is vain.
免我虛度。

# 4

I need Thee every hour,
我每時刻需你，
Teach me Thy will;
我今懇求；
And Thy rich promises
祢所應許福氣，
In me fulfill.
向我成就。

# 5

I need Thee every hour,
我每時刻需祢，
Most Holy One;
至聖恩主；
Oh, make me Thine indeed,
使我完全屬祢，
Thou blessed Son.
永偕無阻。
